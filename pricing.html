Contract terms

The standard terms, stated upfront

These appear in every fixed-scope agreement. They exist so a stalled project has a defined consequence instead of an argument.

  1. Data within five business days of kickoff

    You provide the complete agreed dataset within five business days. Analysis cannot start on a partial extract without changing the estimate.

  2. Feedback within three business days

    Review milestones are returned within three business days so the schedule holds for both sides.

  3. One or two revision rounds, defined in the scope

    Revisions cover corrections and refinements to the agreed deliverable. New questions are new scope.

  4. Change orders for new scope

    Additional analyses, extra endpoints or new data sources are quoted as a written change order and approved before work begins.

  5. Day-for-day extension on client delays

    If data or feedback arrives late, the delivery date moves by the same number of days. No penalty, no surprise.

  6. Confidentiality by default

    We sign your NDA, or provide ours. Your data is not reused, resold or included in any public case study without written permission.

Pricing questions

Before you ask

Why publish prices at all?

Because the first question every lab manager has is whether this fits the budget, and a range answers it in ten seconds. The written scope you receive names one number, not a range.

What happens if the scope changes mid-project?

We issue a change order describing the new work and its price, and you approve it before anything is built. The original scope stays fixed until you say otherwise.

Do you take equity, milestones or deferred payment?

No. Fixed-price engagements are invoiced with a deposit at kickoff and the balance on delivery. Retainers are invoiced monthly in advance.

Are the prices in Canadian dollars?

Yes, all figures are Canadian dollars and exclude applicable taxes. US clients are invoiced in CAD unless we agree otherwise in writing.
