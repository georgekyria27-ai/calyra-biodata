Services

Analysis, pipelines and data QC — scoped as fixed deliverables

Three service lines built around one observation: labs under about fifty people generate more data than they can defensibly analyse, and cannot justify a permanent data hire.

01 — Lead service

Biostatistics & statistical analysis

For teams that have the data and the biology, and need the statistics to hold up under review.

Discuss a study

What we deliver

  • Study design review and sample-size or power justification, written so it can go into a grant or protocol
  • Statistical analysis plan documenting endpoints, models and handling of missing data before analysis begins
  • Regression, ANOVA, mixed-effects and repeated-measures models with assumption checks shown, not assumed
  • Survival and time-to-event analysis, including Kaplan–Meier estimates and Cox models
  • Publication-ready tables and figures, plus a methods and results section written in plain scientific English
  • Commented R, Python or SAS scripts that reproduce every number in the report

Typical clients

Academic research groups, graduate students facing a thesis committee, small biotech teams without a statistician, and clinics analysing their own outcome data.

Typical trigger

A reviewer asked how the sample size was justified, or a dataset has been sitting analysed-but-unwritten for months.

02 — Lead service

Bioinformatics support

Sequencing output turned into interpretable biology, without hiring a full-time bioinformatician.

Discuss a dataset

What we deliver

  • Bulk RNA-seq processing and differential expression, from raw reads through annotated result tables
  • DNA methylation and epigenetic array or sequencing analysis
  • Microbiome and metagenomic profiling with diversity and composition analysis
  • Variant calling and annotation workflows for targeted or whole-genome data
  • Proteomics and phosphoproteomics quantification support
  • Figure sets built for manuscripts and investor decks — volcano plots, heatmaps, PCA, pathway enrichment
  • Documented, re-runnable pipelines so the next batch does not start from zero

Typical clients

Small biotechs and diagnostics companies generating sequencing data faster than they can analyse it, and core facilities needing overflow capacity.

Typical trigger

A sequencing run has landed, the deadline is real, and the one person who knows the pipeline has left or is booked solid.

03

QA / QC data review

A second set of eyes on the data and documentation you are about to stand behind.

Request a review

What we deliver

  • Systematic dataset review for transcription errors, unit inconsistencies, duplicated records and impossible values
  • Control-chart and trend review to surface drifting instruments or shifting baselines
  • Method validation report review against the acceptance criteria you have committed to
  • SOP review for clarity, internal consistency and whether the written procedure matches what the bench actually does
  • A written findings log ranked by risk, with concrete remediation steps

Typical clients

Small contract labs, environmental and materials testing firms, and CROs needing independent overflow QA during a busy quarter.

Boundaries

What we do not do

Being clear about this saves everyone a call.

No wet-lab work

No sample handling, no assays, no instrument time. We work downstream of the bench.

No clinical advice

We analyse data. We do not diagnose, treat, or make medical recommendations about individuals.

No regulatory sign-off

We support submissions and audits with analysis and documentation. Accountability stays with your quality unit.
