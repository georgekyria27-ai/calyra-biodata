Statistics for FDA submissions

Regulatory-grade statistics for FDA submissions, from first study design to the statistical sections of a submission package.

Book a scoping call

What we deliver

We work alongside your clinical, regulatory and engineering teams so the numbers in the package are planned up front, documented, and reproducible. This is medical device biostatistics — biostatistics for FDA filings, including 510(k) statistics — not a filing service: we do not file the submission or decide what to claim.

  • Study design.

    Endpoints, comparators, randomization and blinding approaches, and analysis populations, defined before data collection starts.

  • Statistical analysis plans (SAPs) and SAP review for medical devices.

    We write statistical analysis plans and review existing SAPs: prespecified analyses, missing-data handling, multiplicity and interim analyses, written so a reviewer can follow every step.

  • Sample size and power.

    Documented calculations with stated assumptions and sensitivity scenarios you can defend in review.

  • Data QC for clinical and device trial data.

    Independent checks of trial datasets, traceable data-to-table workflows, and a QC record for every table, listing and figure prepared for the submission package.

  • Diagnostic-accuracy and device validation analyses.

    Sensitivity, specificity, PPV/NPV, ROC/AUC, and agreement and reliability analyses (Bland–Altman, kappa, ICC), with confidence intervals and prespecified acceptance criteria.

  • Statistical sections and analyses supporting 510(k), De Novo and PMA submissions.

    Write-ups and outputs prepared for your regulatory lead to include in the package. We do not file the submission or decide what to claim.

Typical clients

Medical-device and diagnostics teams preparing a study or a 510(k), De Novo or PMA package, and sponsors who need the statistical sections planned, analysed or QC'd before a filing.

Typical trigger

A device study is being designed for a submission, or a package is coming together and the statistical sections still need to be planned, analysed or QC'd.

Related services

Study design review, sample-size justification and statistical analysis plans for studies that are not going into an FDA package are part of biostatistics and statistical analysis. Independent checks of lab datasets and documentation are covered under QA / QC data review and data-integrity and documentation review.
