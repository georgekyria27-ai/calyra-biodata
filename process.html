How we work

A defensible process, from first call to handover

Analytics consulting fails in predictable ways: vague scope, undocumented methods, and a deliverable nobody can re-run. Each step below exists to close one of those gaps.

Engagement flow

Six steps, no surprises

Most projects move from first call to signed scope in under two weeks.

Start at step one

  1. Scoping call — 30 minutes

    We ask what data you generate, where it currently goes, what reporting takes too long, and what decision is being delayed. If we are not the right fit, we say so on this call.

  2. Written scope and fixed price

    Within a few business days you receive a scope document: deliverables, method summary, assumptions, timeline, price and what is explicitly excluded. Nothing starts before you approve it.

  3. Analysis plan before data

    Once signed, we write the analysis plan — endpoints, statistical or pipeline methods, handling of missing data, figure list. You approve it. This is what stops methods from being chosen to suit results.

  4. Secure data intake

    Data is transferred by whatever channel your policy requires, logged with a provenance record, and held only for the agreed duration under NDA.

  5. Analysis, with a checkpoint

    Work proceeds against the approved plan, with a mid-project checkpoint so you see intermediate output early rather than a finished report you disagree with.

  6. Review, revision, handover

    You receive the deliverable, the scripts, the written methods and a walkthrough session. Revision rounds run inside the agreed window, then the project closes cleanly.

Quality control

Four controls on every deliverable

These are not aspirations. They are gates — work does not ship until each one is satisfied.

  • Written plan first. No analysis begins without an approved plan naming the methods.
  • Reproducible code always. Every figure and table regenerates from raw input by a script you keep.
  • Peer review where stakes are high. Regulatory, sponsor-facing and publication work is checked by a second qualified analyst.
  • Fixed pre-delivery checklist. Version, provenance, assumption checks, figure legends and file formats verified before release.

Staffing

Who does the work, and how they are chosen

Calyra BioData runs a specialist bench rather than a fixed department. You are told who is assigned to your project before it begins, and what they have done before.

Vetting

Portfolio and test project

Every analyst is assessed on prior work, must demonstrate they can write an analysis plan before touching data, and completes a small paid test project before being put on a client engagement.

Continuity

A bench, not one person

Two to three vetted specialists are maintained per service line, so one person's availability never becomes your missed deadline.

Accountability

One point of contact

You deal with the practice, not a marketplace. Scoping, quality control and delivery responsibility stay in one place regardless of who executes.
